# How I mapped my project to the role

I used this posting to choose the workflows I wanted to demonstrate. My implementation and its completion boundaries are recorded below.

Target: Analyst, Retail E-Commerce, Tim Hortons, Canada at Restaurant Brands International. Toronto; full-time; five days onsite. The posting gives a base salary range of CAD $70,000–$90,000 and mentions a discretionary bonus. It seeks roughly 1–3 years of relevant experience and postsecondary education or equivalent practical experience.

Source: https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/4483176791/ (reviewed October 26, 2026).

| Role requirement, paraphrased | Project evidence | Completion boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Maintain online merchandise and check the shopping experience | Catalog editor, price/content checks, collection filters, storefront, cart, checkout | Local workflow works; actual Shopify admin practice remains |
| Coordinate releases and promotional activity | Launch owners, deadlines, dependencies, approvals, local publication, MAPLE10 promotion | No real company campaign or store changes |
| Investigate availability and fulfillment problems | Stockout queue, seven-day demand, stock receipts, shipping queue, owner handoffs | No supplier purchase orders or carrier contact |
| Analyze trading results in Excel and dashboards | SQL calculations, date filters, channel/product reporting, XLSX formulas and charts | Native PivotTable must be created in Excel using the included steps/script |
| Organize creative material and samples | Asset library, sample/shoot status, usage rights, approvals and replacements | Illustrations replace actual photography in the synthetic scenario |
| Communicate across teams and improve routines | Team assignments, launch gates, audit trail, operating review, SOP and timed demo | Demonstrates process design rather than actual team management |
| Preferred platform and analytics exposure | Shopify draft CSV and read-only GraphQL adapter; GA4 report adapter; CRM segments and Klaviyo previews | Credentials, platform permissions and live behavior remain unverified |

I use this project as evidence of applied skills. I discuss my employment experience and education separately, without treating project work as additional years of paid experience.
